playdoink

Clever, hand-made games by Craig Hillman.

  • Quadruplex: untangle two hidden words in each of four rows; the leftover letters answer the clue. A new puzzle every day.
  • Duck, Duck, Goose: eight eggs share a hidden rule and one is the goose. Crack all eight ducks without hitting the goose.
  • Doink!: the original playdoink game.
