About

playdoink is a small home for games by Craig Hillman.

Craig has been a dyed-in-the-wool word game fanatic since Boggle. You may know him from when he owned the leaderboard on your local dive bar's Megatouch Photo Hunt.

Craig designs every game here and writes every puzzle by hand. There's no generator behind Quadruplex or Duck, Duck, Goose: each day's puzzle is one he made, tested and chose.

That's the idea behind the site. Instead of a pile of unrelated games, it's one person's taste in one place. If you like one of his games, there's a good chance you'll like the next one too.

All games on playdoink.com have been subject to editorial approval.

More games are on the way. Sign up and we'll let you know when they land.
