# Do!nk style guide

## Game names

- Always all caps, in the **Luckiest Guy** font.
- The I in DOINK is an exclamation point: **DO!NK** (never "Doink" or "DOINK" on screen).
- Colours:
  - **MEGA**DO!NK: MEGA in blue `#2b8bf6` (Do!nk's popup blue), DO!NK in black `#000`.
  - **ULTRA**DO!NK: ULTRA in red `#e00000`, DO!NK in black `#000`.
  - Plain DO!NK: as on its splash screen.
- A "BETA!" tag, where used, is red `#e00000`, small, after the name.

Source: Craig's "MEGADO!NK notes" email, Nov 12, 2026.
